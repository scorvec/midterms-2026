State legislatures, 2026

← Congress and governors

Chances of control of fourteen competitive state legislative chambers.

How it works: each seat's expected margin is the district's 2024 presidential lean (precinct returns joined to legislative districts), the national environment from the generic ballot, incumbency and the state's own Senate / governor polling, with errors that move every seat in a state together and with the national mood. Multi-member districts elect their top candidates. Sources and backtest: about the forecast.
